Start a meeting now

No account needed. Meetings start at 30 minutes, and near the end you can add 30 more, up to 90 minutes. Up to 8 people. You get the notes by email.

Everyone will get a link to the meeting and the saved files afterwards.

Trouble joining? Content blockers, strict privacy settings, Private Relay or Lockdown Mode can stop video meetings from loading, especially in Safari. Try Chrome or Firefox, or turn off blockers for meet.example.org.

Start meeting later?

Planning ahead? Choose your date and time.

Pick a day on the calendar.

Everyone gets an invitation with a calendar file, and, after the meeting is complete, a private summary. Scheduled meetings run up to 90 minutes.

First time scheduling? We will email you a link to confirm before any invitations go out.

Hosts: You can start your meeting up to 15 minutes before it begins. Login using the email address your invitation was sent to.

Joining someone else's meeting?

Just open the link your host sent you. Or type the room name here.

All meetings are recorded, saved, transcribed, and summarized by AI. By joining or starting a meeting you agree to be recorded. Recordings are kept for up to 1 month and a private link is emailed to participants.
